# Wallpaper sources

Every image here was taken from a public collection whose repository states a
licence that permits redistribution (named below), converted to JPEG (at most
3840 px wide, metadata stripped). The repositories' licences cover their own
contents as published; the artwork is by the original artists. If you are an
artist or rights holder and want an image removed, open an issue and it will
be deleted.

Skipped on purpose: images of third-party characters, products or brands
(anime fan art, car makers, film franchises), paintings still in copyright, and
images carrying a project's logo.

The three files in this folder that are not listed below (`artificial-valley`,
`astronaut`, `catppuccin-spiderman`) predate this list.
